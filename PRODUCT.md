## Stack

Existing WordPress site (scma-kvk.com.ua) on the Divi theme. The homepage is a Divi builder page (ID 212621); new sections are delivered as Divi fullwidth code modules (HTML + scoped CSS + small vanilla JS), plus the native Divi blog module (news) and Divi contact form. Front-end runs many custom "SCMA …" optimisation plugins (page cache, webp rewrite, lazy loading, URL normalisation). No build step.

## Users

All four groups, confirmed by the owner:
- Farmers and agro-holdings: need truck scales (typically 18 m / 80 t) for grain and harvest control; eligible for the state 25% compensation.
- Mining, metallurgy and heavy industry (GOKs, ArcelorMittal, Metinvest, DTEK): long-running industrial weighing, automation.
- Logistics and construction (quarries, concrete plants, logistics yards).
- Dealers and existing weighing companies looking for a manufacturer partner.

They arrive from Google or a referral, usually decide with a phone call or a request for a consultation.

## Product Purpose

Kryvyi Rih Weighing Company (SCMA-KVK, «Криворізька Вагова Компанія») manufactures and installs TITAN truck scales (platforms 6–22 m, 30–100 t), plus railway, conveyor and platform scales, and services them (calibration, diagnostics, repair, installation/dismantling). The homepage succeeds when a visitor calls +38 (067) 632-67-98 or leaves a consultation request.

## Positioning

Owner-confirmed differentiators, in order:
1. Own production plus turnkey installation anywhere in Ukraine, from 3 days, from foundation to verification.
2. 50+ years of engineering school: industrial automation, 40+ patented developments, 26 exhibition awards.
3. State compensation −25% on TITAN scales (company is in the Ministry of Economy order as a supplier to the agro sector).

## Operating Context

Scales live outdoors on farms, quarries and industrial yards: steel platforms with orange wheel guards, concrete foundations, weighing terminals with digital readouts, trucks rolling on. Buyers are owners, chief engineers and procurement managers, often reading on a phone in the field or on a desktop in an office.
